Who we are

THE 2JS
STANDARD.

Automotive care built around attention, thoughtful processes and a respect for the details.

01 / The idea

Good detailing
is a discipline.

2JS Detailing brings together a range of automotive care services, from paint correction and decontamination to ceramic coatings, paint protection film and interior detailing. The aim is simple: help every customer find the right care for their vehicle and the finish they want to achieve.

Rather than treating every vehicle the same, the team welcomes specific requests and can shape a service combination around individual preferences. That conversation is where good detailing begins.

02 / How we think
