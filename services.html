01 / Find your finish

One vehicle.
Many ways to care.

2JS offers a broad range of automotive detailing treatments. The right combination depends on your vehicle's current condition, how you use it and the finish you want to maintain. Speak to the team for guidance and a tailored quotation.

REFINEMENT / 01

Paint correction

Machine polishing and paint refinement to improve gloss and reduce the visible appearance of swirl marks and suitable paint defects.

PROTECTION / 02

Ceramic coatings

Coating options for vehicle paint as well as rims, leather, fabric and trim, selected according to the surface and intended use.

DEFENCE / 03

Paint protection film

Clear protective film options for paintwork, with interior PPF also listed among 2JS services. Discuss coverage and suitability with the team.

INTERIOR / 04

Interior detailing

Care for cabin surfaces, seating, carpets and trim, with leather, fabric and trim coating options available.

CLARITY / 05

Windscreen protection film

Protection film for windscreens. Ask the team about vehicle compatibility, coverage and the options available.

FINISHING / 06

Rims & calipers

Rim and caliper detailing, plus rim ceramic coating options to complement your vehicle's finish.

PREPARATION / 07

Paint decontamination

A surface-preparation service that helps remove bonded contaminants before selected refinement or protection treatments.

DETAILING / 08

Engine bay & packages

Engine bay detailing and paint-and-interior packages are also listed by 2JS. Custom requests can be discussed with the team.

02 / Personalised care

Made around

Not every vehicle needs every treatment. A daily driver, a weekend car and a vehicle that spends long periods outdoors can have very different priorities. 2JS can accommodate specific requests and focus on areas that matter to you.

  • Discuss your goals and budget before selecting a treatment.
  • Ask which surfaces and coverage areas are included in a quote.
  • Confirm preparation requirements, product options and aftercare guidance with the team.
